# Top 20 Difficult Investor / Judge Questions

Best possible answers for OSTX presentation. Keep answers crisp; point to live product surfaces when possible.

---

### 1. Isn’t this just ChatGPT wrapping a pitch deck?
**Answer:** No. Kulima separates recommendation scoring from Evidence Integrity. Live demos show contradictions, unsupported claims, sparse-mode disclosure, and syndicate dissent — artifacts a generic chat summarizer does not produce or persist into IC exports.

### 2. What if OpenAI / search APIs are down during a pilot?
**Answer:** Offline Demo Mode activates automatically. The orchestrator falls back to the OSTX validation template so the IC workflow continues. Pilots can also explore the pre-seeded dataset with zero credits.

### 3. How do you avoid hallucinated diligence?
**Answer:** Evidence Integrity is a first-class object: grades, contradictions, unsupported claims, verification checklists. Confidence can be reduced by integrity findings; sparse cases are labeled, not padded.

### 4. Why should an investor trust your Trust Score?
**Answer:** Trust is grounded in source attribution and graph corroboration, then shown beside integrity grade and depth. We never present a lone vanity number — AgriNova (A/88) vs SolarHarvest (F/32) is the proof.

### 5. Does OBSERVE mean the model is uncertain?
**Answer:** OBSERVE is a capital decision, not a shrug. GreenLink shows operating signal with blocking verification items and time-boxed milestones. Uncertainty is expressed via confidence + integrity, not by collapsing to OBSERVE by default.

### 6. Can founders game this with better SEO / more web pages?
**Answer:** More pages without corroboration still fail consistency checks. SolarHarvest-style cases with thin, conflicting evidence remain PASS. High-authority institutional sources outweigh promotional pages.

### 7. What’s your data moat?
**Answer:** The moat is the closed-loop investor OS: multi-agent diligence → integrity engine → twin syndicate → thesis fit → persistent runs → IC exports → portfolio analytics. Competitors may score; Kulima runs the committee workflow.

### 8. How is this different from Affinity / PitchBook / Harmonic?
**Answer:** Those systems excel at CRM, data aggregation, or sourcing. Kulima is decision infrastructure for Africa-focused IC: evidence integrity, Africa thesis engine, and syndicate simulation on top of deal intelligence.

### 9. Is Twin Syndicate just role-play?
**Answer:** It is a structured multi-persona committee layer that produces votes, dissent, blocking concerns, and a consensus thesis stored with the brief. Judges can inspect dissent on GreenLink vs unanimous PASS on SolarHarvest.

### 10. What’s your false-positive risk on INVEST?
**Answer:** INVEST requires strength across score, trust, and integrity. AgriNova clears that bar; GreenLink does not despite being a “good story.” Integrity conflicts and red flags gate conviction.

### 11. What’s your false-negative risk on PASS?
**Answer:** PASS triggers on critical verification failure, not on missing polish. If SolarHarvest later produces ARENE licenses and audited financials, a new run can reopen the decision. We optimize for capital protection first.

### 12. Do you replace human analysts?
**Answer:** No. Kulima compresses first-pass OSINT and IC packaging so partners spend time on judgment, references, and structuring. Next steps and verification checklists are explicit handoffs to humans.

### 13. How do coverage metrics work?
**Answer:** Evidence Coverage ≈ sources relative to claims; Signal Coverage ≈ completeness of core agent sections. Analytics reads stored runs only — no mock inflation. Sparse PASS cases intentionally lower cohort coverage.

### 14. Is the product Africa-specific or generically global?
**Answer:** Thesis and research posture are Africa-first (markets, corridors, DFI/CVC archetypes), but the integrity + IC workflow generalizes. Depth in African OSINT and thesis fit is the wedge.

### 15. What’s the business model?
**Answer:** Pilot seats for funds/accelerators → per-seat or firm license for Kulima OS → expansion into portfolio monitoring and IC pack automation. Demo proves workflow value before enterprise procurement.

### 16. Who is the buyer and who is the user?
**Answer:** Buyer: managing partner / head of platform / innovation lead. Users: analysts and associates who run diligence and assemble IC packs daily.

### 17. How do you handle private documents and secrets?
**Answer:** Document upload paths exist for diligence packs; access is authenticated. We do not claim unrestricted private-data ingestion in the OSTX demo — the validation cases prove the public-evidence path first.

### 18. What takes this from demo to production pilot next week?
**Answer:** Seeded dataset + offline fallback + report downloads + URL-synced workspaces are live. Pilot onboarding is: invite users, load their first 10 deals into Runs, export IC memos, collect feedback ratings.

### 19. Why will a fund switch from their current Notion + GPT workflow?
**Answer:** Because Notion+GPT does not persist integrity grades, contradiction ledgers, syndicate dissent, thesis status, or portfolio coverage analytics tied to each run. Kulima makes those objects durable and reviewable.

### 20. What’s the single biggest risk to your company?
**Answer:** Distribution and trust calibration — funds must believe PASS/OBSERVE decisions are valuable, not just INVEST theater. That is why the OSTX pack leads with all three outcomes and shows the evidence path for each.
