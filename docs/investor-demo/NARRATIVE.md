# Investor Demo Narrative

How Kulima OS differentiates the three OSTX validation cases.

---

## 1) AgriNova Malawi — Why INVEST (Trust 88)

### Why this score
AgriNova combines **strong founder proof**, **corroborated traction**, and **clean evidence integrity**. The Trust Score sits in the high band because institutional sources (USAID partner feature, ministry registry, payments partner) reinforce the same story. Confidence is High because the corpus is deep and consistent.

### Why not OBSERVE or PASS
- No material contradictions
- Unit economics and retention narrative are supported
- Thesis match is high (AgTech + Seed + SADC corridor)
- Syndicate majority is INVEST (only mild CO-INVEST dissent on strategic structure)

### Evidence that drove the decision
1. Audited / partner-featured revenue growth ($1.2M ARR, 310% YoY)
2. Verified cooperative / cold-chain operating footprint
3. Founder track record tied to USAID Southern Africa operations
4. Integrity Grade A with empty contradiction set
5. Twin Syndicate consensus thesis favoring allocation

### One-line investor takeaway
> This is what “IC-ready” looks like when evidence quality and commercial strength move together.

---

## 2) GreenLink Foods — Why OBSERVE (Trust 64)

### Why this score
GreenLink is a **real operator story with unresolved proof gaps**. Trust lands mid-band because some registry/website evidence exists, but a medium-severity contradiction and unsupported yield claims prevent conviction. Confidence is Medium: enough signal to engage, not enough to underwrite.

### Why not INVEST
- Retail partner count conflict (30 claimed vs 12 verified)
- Structural power-grid / diesel cost risk
- Customer concentration
- Syndicate dissent: DFI wants impact INVEST; Global Tier-1 leans PASS

### Why not PASS
- Business is operating (not fabricated-looking)
- Sector thesis remains attractive (urban fresh produce)
- Clear, time-boxed milestones can change the decision

### Evidence that drove the decision
1. Contradiction on supermarket contract count
2. Founder interview admitting diesel cost pressure
3. Integrity Grade C / conflicts status
4. Watchlist recommendation with solar hybrid gating milestone

### One-line investor takeaway
> OBSERVE is not indecision — it is disciplined capital waiting for verification.

---

## 3) SolarHarvest Cooperative — Why PASS (Trust 32)

### Why this score
SolarHarvest fails the **minimum evidence bar**. Trust collapses because the core asset claim (government mini-grid concession) is absent from ARENE public records, corporate registration is unverified, and the corpus is sparse. Confidence is Low by design.

### Why not OBSERVE
- Blocking legal/regulatory gaps (not just operational milestones)
- Integrity Grade F and sparse-mode disclosure
- Unanimous syndicate PASS with near-zero productive dissent
- Thesis engine status BLOCK on evidence fit

### Why recommendation differs from GreenLink
GreenLink has mixed-but-real operating evidence and fixable diligence items.  
SolarHarvest has **non-corroborated foundational claims** — the kind that should stop an IC cold.

### Evidence that drove the decision
1. Deck claim of exclusive concession vs ARENE gazette non-match
2. Unsupported subscriber and financial statements
3. Critical red flags on concession + registration
4. Thin source count triggering limited-coverage posture

### One-line investor takeaway
> Kulima’s edge is not optimism — it is the ability to say PASS with receipts.
