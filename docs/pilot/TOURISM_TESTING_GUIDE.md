# Kulima FLEX — Tourism SME Testing Guide

You run a lodge, a tour operator, a destination business. Your decisions are
fast, seasonal and reversible — pricing before high season, solar before the
rains, staffing across the quiet months. The platform must **keep up with
you**, not turn a two-day decision into a two-week study.

## What to Upload (keep it light — this is the point)

- Last two years of occupancy/revenue summaries (even a spreadsheet print)
- The bank or investor document you already have
- A community/conservancy agreement or park-fee schedule
- One page of plans: renovation, expansion, new package

## Decisions to Run Through the Platform

1. **Seasonality capex** — solar micro-grid, renovation timing, boat purchase.
2. **Pricing / packaging change** against visitor-economy signals.
3. **Low-season staffing** commitment.
4. **Conservancy or community partnership spend.**

## What to Verify (mark ✓ / ✗ / note)

- The output is a **Tourism Resilience Report**, not an investment pitch
  review: no "founder", no "cap table", no IC-committee wording. ✗-tag any
  of that as INC-3.
- **Tourism, climate and community signals actually move the verdict.**
  Check the Signals page: energy/climate activity, community income markers
  and destination/tourism evidence should appear with real sources (your
  documents, or clearly-labelled open research). Then ask the AI Analyst:
  "Would this recommendation change if the community signal disappeared?" —
  the decision dossier weights tourism/climate/community heavily for your
  type; a shrug is a finding.
- **Speed:** from upload to a decision you'd defend, the clock target is
  days, not weeks — and using the platform must not slow a 48-hour decision.
  Note where you waited on the system (FR-1 with seconds).
- **Evidence honesty:** thin documents should produce a modest trust score
  and visible gaps. If the platform is confidently praising a business your
  own uploads don't support — that is INC-1, report it same-day.

## The 30-Day Test That Matters (from your phone, in under a minute)

When the **⏳ Follow-Up Due** chip appears — on the workspace header, your dashboard,
or the **30-Day Follow-ups Waiting** list in `/outcomes`:

- One tap: **✓ Decision taken / ↻ Decision in progress / No decision yet.** Done.
  Ten seconds. "No decision yet" is a real, useful answer — record it, don't skip it.
- When the season tells you whether it worked, open **Update**: was the recommendation
  followed? what happened? rate the outcome
  (Successful / Partially / Unsuccessful) and save.
- Then in `/outcomes` open the row and fill **What Happened · What Was Predicted ·
  What Was Missed · What Worked · What Failed** → **Save Outcome & Lessons**.
- The **Outcome Review PDF** is what your bank sees when you ask for the next
  facility — decisions with receipts beat decisions with memory.

## Your Season Calendar (short cycle — this is your advantage)

| When | Act | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Upload what you already have: occupancy/revenue summaries, the bank paper, one page of plans | Trust score reflects thin evidence — that's correct |
| Day 1 | Read `/decision`: reason · evidence · confidence · next action. Download the **Tourism Resilience Report** | Report open on your phone |
| Day 2 | **Make the decision** (price, staffing, solar, renovation) | Note it in the comment field |
| Day 30 | One-tap follow-up | `✓ Follow-up recorded` |
| Day 60 | **Update**, comment prefixed `D60:` — bookings tell you by now | Comment trail |
| End of season | Rate the outcome + lessons; export **Outcome Review** | PDF ready for the bank |

If any step takes longer than a minute on a phone, that is `FR-1` — tell your mentor
how long it actually took.

## What You Hand Back

1. Every one-tap answer you gave — including "no decision yet".
2. At least one **rated** outcome with a written lesson in your own words.
3. The Outcome Review PDF.
4. Where you waited on the system (with seconds).

## Your Unique Value to the Pilot

Tourism outcomes resolve fast — you will be the first cohort with *rated*
results at day 90. Your one-tap answers, honest or not, are the most
requested data in this programme. "We tried the rate change, bookings fell,
we reverted" is exactly the sentence the system learns from.
