# Kulima FLEX — NGO Pilot Playbook

You are testing whether an evidence platform helps the decisions your board
and donors actually ask you to justify. Use your real documents — the system
is designed for annual reports, programme reviews and M&E reports, not pitch
decks. Development programmes have their own playbook:
[DEVELOPMENT_PROGRAMME_PLAYBOOK.md](DEVELOPMENT_PROGRAMME_PLAYBOOK.md).

## What to Upload (pick the decision you face next quarter)

- Annual report (financials + narrative + M&E annex)
- A programme review or end-of-phase evaluation
- A donor agreement or concept note under negotiation
- Theory of change / logframe export

## Decisions to Run Through the Platform

1. **Grant renewal or redesign** — does the capacity review surface what the
   funder will probe?
2. **Implementing partner selection / continuation** — partnership strength
   and delivery evidence.
3. **Programme scale-up vs consolidation** across counties.
4. **Donor concentration response** — walk away, diversify, or double down?

## What to Verify (mark ✓ / ✗ / note in your mentor's checklist)

- The review speaks your language: **Institutional Capacity** — six dimensions
  (Institutional Capacity · Programme Performance · Community Reach · Funding
  Sustainability · Partnership Strength · Monitoring Quality). No "founder
  gap", no "investment drivers", no IC-committee framing. ✗-tag any venture
  language as INC-3.
- **Community reach**: your beneficiary numbers and geography appear as
  evidence items attributed to your uploaded documents (source labelled as
  your document, never as "open research").
- **Funding**: budget-vs-received gaps and donor concentration appear as
  risks — if your finance lead says "we already knew that", check whether the
  platform quantified it the same way.
- **Partnerships**: MOUs, county-government counterparts, consortium roles
  surfaced from the report text.
- **Implementation capacity**: milestone completion, MEAL/logframe coverage —
  does the delivery evidence match your own MEAL reporting? Rate the match
  1–5 (this validates the evidence chain against your system of record).
- **AI Analyst**: ask "What evidence is weak?" — it must admit what your
  documents don't prove (e.g., outcome claims without baseline data).
- **Reports**: give the Decision & Capacity Memo to someone who didn't
  upload anything. Can they identify the organisation correctly? Any single
  statement they cannot trace to a document?

## The 30-Day Test That Matters

Thirty days after the assessment, when the follow-up reminder appears:

- Time yourself. It should take **under 30 seconds** to answer honestly.
- Answer in the words your board would use. "Partially followed" is a good
  answer. "Not yet" is a good answer.
- At day 60/90 press **Update** on the same record (comment starts `D60:`).
- When the grant/partner decision resolves, rate the outcome and fill the five lesson
  fields (**What Happened / What Was Predicted / What Was Missed / What Worked / What
  Failed**) → **Save Outcome & Lessons**, then export the
  **Outcome Review Report** — that PDF is your board's proof that decisions
  here are closed-loop.

## Your 90-Day Calendar (one real decision)

| When | Who | Act | Proof it happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 0–2 | Evidence holder | Upload annual report + programme review + donor agreement + logframe export; confirm the detected profile is **NGO** | `/evidence` shows each document with its own trust line |

## What You Hand Back at Day 90

1. The **Institutional Capacity Review** you circulated.
2. The **Outcome Review Report** PDF for the closed decision.
3. The comment trail (`D60:` / `D90:`) showing what actually happened.
4. Your incident list — especially every sentence in any report that claimed more
   than your documents support.
5. A one-line answer to: *did the platform change the decision, speed it up, or
   strengthen how you defended it?* If the honest answer is "none of these", say so —
   that is the most useful finding in the programme.

## Judge Honesty, Not Flattery

A low trust score on a thin document set is a **correct** result. An
"INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE" banner where you expected a success rate is a **pass**,
not a bug. Your most valuable finding is any sentence in any report that
claims more than your documents support — flag it as INC-1 the same day.
