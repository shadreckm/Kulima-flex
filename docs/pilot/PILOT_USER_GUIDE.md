# Kulima FLEX — Pilot User Guide

A one-page-per-step guide for pilot organisations. Everything described here
exists today — no roadmap items.

## What Kulima FLEX Does

You upload real documents about a real decision. Kulima turns them into
evidence, scores the evidence honestly, surfaces signals and risks, gives you
a recommendation you can question, produces board-ready reports — and then
comes back in 30 days to ask **what actually happened**. That last step is the
point of the whole system: your answer is what makes the next assessment
smarter.

## Start Here (one flow)

1. **Upload** — Landing page (`/`): drag in your documents (PDF/DOCX/TXT:
   annual report, programme review, pitch deck, feasibility study…). The
   platform detects what kind of entity you are: NGO, Development Programme,
   Government Programme, Tourism SME, or Startup — and speaks your language
   from then on.
2. **Assessment runs** — watch progress in the workspace (`/flex`). When it
   completes, scores and a recommendation appear.
3. **Read the evidence first** — `/evidence`: what was extracted, what was
   attributed to your uploaded documents, what could not be verified.
   Low trust scores are honest scores, not bugs.
4. **Signals** — `/signals`: risk and opportunity signals, each labelled with
   its source (uploaded document or open research).
5. **Decision** — `/decision`: the full dossier — recommendation tier, trust
   score, risks, next steps. **Make your real decision here** and note it.
6. **Reports** — `/reports`: download the memo / capacity review / one-pager
   for your board, donors or committee. Every report carries the same score
   and the same red flags — no page disagrees with another.
7. **Ask the Analyst** — `/flex` (AI Analyst): challenge the recommendation.
   "Why was this recommendation made?" · "What evidence supports it?" ·
   "What evidence is weak?" · "What changed?" — every answer cites sources;
   when it has no evidence it says so.

## The 30-Day Follow-Up (your most valuable act)

Thirty days after your assessment, a reminder appears where you naturally
work — the workspace header, the Dashboard, Reports, the AI Analyst:

- **⏳ Follow-Up Due** — click it once. Answer in one tap:
  **✓ Decision taken · ↻ In progress · ✕ No decision yet**. Under ten seconds.
- Later, refine with the full update: was the recommendation followed?
  comments? outcome (Successful / Partially / Unsuccessful).
- Re-answering is safe — it updates the same record. Use it at day 60 and 90
  (prefix comments `D60:` / `D90:` so history reads clearly).
- When the outcome is rated, every surface shows the recorded review — what
  happened, lessons — and you can export the **Outcome Review Report** PDF
  for your board.

## What "NO DATA" and "INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE" Mean

Kulima never invents numbers. Calibration claims appear only when enough
rated outcomes actually exist. Early in a pilot you will see honest empties —
that is the system telling you the truth, which is the thing you are there to
test.

## Feedback

Anything wrong, unclear or missing: `/feedback` — say exactly what happened
and what you expected. Feedback about over-claiming is especially welcome.
