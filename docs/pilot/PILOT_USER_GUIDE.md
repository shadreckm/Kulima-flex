# Kulima FLEX — Pilot User Guide

A one-page-per-step guide for pilot organisations. Everything described here
exists today — no roadmap items.

## What Kulima FLEX Does

You upload real documents about a real decision. Kulima turns them into
evidence, scores the evidence honestly, surfaces signals and risks, gives you
a recommendation you can question, produces board-ready reports — and then
comes back in 30 days to ask **what actually happened**. That last step is the
point of the whole system: your answer is what makes the next assessment
smarter.

## Start Here (one flow)

1. **Upload** — Landing page (`/`): drag in your documents (PDF/DOCX/TXT:
   annual report, programme review, pitch deck, feasibility study…). The
   platform detects what kind of entity you are: NGO, Development Programme,
   Government Programme, Tourism SME, or Startup — and speaks your language
   from then on.
2. **Assessment runs** — watch progress in the workspace (`/flex`). When it
   completes, scores and a recommendation appear.
3. **Read the evidence first** — `/evidence`: what was extracted, what was
   attributed to your uploaded documents, what could not be verified.
   Low trust scores are honest scores, not bugs.
4. **Signals** — `/signals`: risk and opportunity signals, each labelled with
   its source (uploaded document or open research).
5. **Decision** — `/decision`: the full dossier — recommendation tier, trust
   score, risks, next steps. **Make your real decision here** and note it.
6. **Reports** — `/reports`: download the memo / capacity review / one-pager
   for your board, donors or committee. Every report carries the same score
   and the same red flags — no page disagrees with another.
7. **Ask the Analyst** — `/flex` (AI Analyst): challenge the recommendation.
   "Why was this recommendation made?" · "What evidence supports it?" ·
   "What evidence is weak?" · "What changed?" — every answer cites sources;
   when it has no evidence it says so.

## The 30-Day Follow-Up (your most valuable act)

Thirty days after your assessment, a reminder appears where you naturally
work — the workspace header chip, the Dashboard **Pending Follow-Ups** list, the
assessments list, the AI Analyst strip and the **30-Day Follow-ups Waiting**
queue in `/outcomes`:

- **⏳ Follow-Up Due** — click it once. Answer in one tap:
  **✓ Decision taken · ↻ Decision in progress · No decision yet**. Under ten seconds.
  One-tap records "too early to tell" honestly — it is never counted as "not followed".
- Later, refine with the full update (four fields): **Was a decision taken? · Was the
  recommendation followed? · Comments (optional) · Outcome (if known — optional)**,
  then **Save & done**.
- Then close it properly in `/outcomes`: open the decision row, set the outcome status
  and fill the five lesson fields (**What Happened · What Was Predicted · What Was
  Missed · What Worked · What Failed**) → **Save Outcome & Lessons**.
- Re-answering is safe — it updates the same record. Use it at day 60 and 90
  (prefix comments `D60:` / `D90:` so history reads clearly).
- When the outcome is rated (Successful / Partially Successful / Unsuccessful), every
  surface shows the recorded review — what happened, lessons — and you can export the
  **Outcome Review Report** PDF for your board.

**A decision is only "closed" when all three exist: the follow-up answer, a rated
outcome, and at least one written lesson.** Two out of three is a half-collected
outcome and cannot produce a learning.

## What "NO DATA" and "INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE" Mean

Kulima never invents numbers. Calibration claims appear only when enough
rated outcomes actually exist. Early in a pilot you will see honest empties —
that is the system telling you the truth, which is the thing you are there to
test.

## Who Does What (agree this on day 0)

| Role | Owns | Sits in | Does **not** do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decision owner** (Executive Director / Programme Manager / owner) | the decision and the follow-up answer | `/decision`, follow-up chip | upload everything themselves |
| **Evidence holder** (MEAL / M&E officer) | document quality and the evidence check | `/evidence`, `/signals` | make the verdict |
| **Recorder** (named, one person) | outcome status + five lesson fields | `/outcomes` | write lessons they didn't observe |
| **Mentor** | the calendar, the timing, the incident log | `/outcomes` Pilot Health | click on their behalf |

Name the recorder in writing at kick-off. Unassigned recording is the single
largest cause of missing outcomes.

## Four questions to ask on each screen

Ask them out loud in the meeting; the answers are what the pilot is collecting.

- `/evidence` — *Which of our own documents is being cited, and what did the
  platform say it could not verify?*
- `/signals` — *Why does this signal matter, what evidence supports it, and what
  would change if it disappeared?*
- `/decision` — *What is the reason, the evidence, the confidence and the next
  action — and do we agree with the band?*
- AI Analyst (`/flex`) — *What evidence is weak? What is missing? What changed?
  What happened after this recommendation?* (Every answer must cite something, or
  say that it cannot.)
- `/outcomes` — *Did we do it? Did it work? What do we tell the next programme?*

## Using your own words

The free-text fields are the most valuable data in this pilot. Write the way you
would say it to your board — "we tried the rate change, bookings fell, we reverted"
teaches the system more than "partially successful". Two sentences of honest prose
beat two paragraphs of polished reporting.

## Consent and use

Your documents, your decision records and your outcome notes stay in your
workspace. Case studies and success stories are drafted only from your closed
loops, in your words, and are shared **only after you have approved the exact text**
(see `SUCCESS_STORY_FRAMEWORK.md`). You can withdraw any quotation at any time.

## Feedback

Anything wrong, unclear or missing: `/feedback` — say exactly what happened
and what you expected. Feedback about over-claiming is especially welcome.
